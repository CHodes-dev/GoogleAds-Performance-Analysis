# Project Reflection

Here are a few important points regarding the implementation of this project, based on my personal view.

### The presence of untraceable ‘Null’ values

There are still some ‘Null’ values whose causes cannot be traced, as the data comes from open sources. Although the magnitude of this is relatively low, so it does not significantly affect the analysis results.

What I wish to highlight is the absence of advertising performance data for the keyword ‘_data analytics training_’ in March. This affects the performance of the keyword itself, such as revenue, clicks, impressions and so on, which will in turn affect overall advertising performance.

This is the drawback of public data, it is impossible to determine the cause of such data gaps. In contrast, if this were to occur with real-business data, the presence of null values could be minimised by identifying the data source and understanding the data loading process.

### A variety of metrics and dimensions is required

The dataset only represents ad performance for the Google Ads channel in a single city over a one-year period in 2024. Consequently, the scope for comparison is very limited.

When deciding which ad campaigns to continue or discontinue, a more in-depth analysis is required, taking into account metrics such as the base cost of ad creation, so that profit margins can be determined and compared with the CPA value. Only then can it be determined whether the advert is still worth retaining or not.

The analysis can be improved by having a benchmark based on a previous time period. For example, if there is advert performance data for the previous year, then performance can be compared side-by-side, allowing us to identify which performance improvement initiatives are working well and which are not.

### Lack of business context

The recommendations provided will be heavily influenced by the business context that has been successfully identified. In this analysis, the business context has been constructed subjectively and may not yet fully reflect real-world business practices.

I am aware of several shortcomings in the business context I have established. For example, there is insufficient explanation of the customer journey, particularly the mechanisms in the consideration stage, specifically, whether the customer’s path after clicking an advert involves registration on the website or must go through an admin. This relates to ‘_Leads_’ whether the leads referred to in the data are people who successfully contacted an admin or the sales department, or those who downloaded free materials from the website.

Regarding the increase in revenue observed in November due to the increased frequency and number of adverts uploaded. I actually imagine if the same situation were to occur in a company. If that were the case, it would be necessary to understand why an advert was only uploaded once a month and whether there is a specified minimum number of adverts. Not to mention that evaluation becomes crucial for assessing the performance of adverts after they have been uploaded. It would be advisable to evaluate channel performance at least at the start of each new quarter.

Those are my personal thoughts on the creation of this project, _thank you for reading_.
