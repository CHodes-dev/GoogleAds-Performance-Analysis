# Exploratory Analysis

### Requirements Gathering
Gathering requirements to ensure the exploratory analysis is more focused. EDA is an initial effort to understand how ad performance has fared over the course of a year. The aim is to identify specific patterns within the data and gain an understanding of whether the data can be used to answer stakeholders’ questions.

Objective of Exploratory Analysis: To examine marketing performance via Google Ads based on revenue generated over time, to determine whether there was an increase or decrease in revenue during that year, and to identify which dimensions had the greatest influence on this outcome.

The metrics used are Cost and Sale_Amount, whilst the dimensions under consideration include Ad_Date, Device, and Keyword.

The analysis to be carried out will therefore cover:

- Performance patterns or trends in ad impressions, to identify when the company generates its highest and lowest revenue and how this compares with its costs. The metrics and dimensions used are Cost and Sale_Amount, whilst the dimension used is Ad_Date.
- The device types with the best and worst performance based on the Sale_Amount metric.
- The keywords with the best and worst performance based on the Sale_Amount metric.

### Initial Insight

| Metric - Dimension | Initial insight |
| --- | --- |
| Cost, Sale_Amount - Ad_Date | Advertising costs in November were significantly higher than in previous months. They usually range from $4,000 to $6,000, but this month they rose 77-fold to $478,448. However, this increase in costs also led to a 78-fold rise in revenue. Average revenue in other months was only $37,283.18, whilst in November it rose to $3,278,058. |
| Cost, Sale_Amount - Ad_Date | Following an increase in costs and revenue in November, both costs and revenue fell by 99% in December. Advertising costs fell sharply this month, with a reduction of $473,068. Consequently, revenue totalled just $38,295, down by at least $3.2 million from the previous month. |
| Cost, Sale_Amount - Ad_Date | Revenue was at its lowest in July, totalling just $24,141. Advertising costs for that month were also among the lowest, at $4,145. |
| Sale_Amount - Device | Generally, there is no significant difference in the revenue generated by customers using different devices to view adverts. Users who clicked on adverts via desktop devices contributed the most revenue, at 34.3%, equivalent to $1,264,233. Meanwhile, the lowest contribution came from users accessing adverts via tablet devices, at around 32.2%, equivalent to $1,188,131. The difference between the highest and lowest figures was only $76,102. |
| Sale_Amount - Keyword | Similarly, there is no significant difference in the revenue generated by each keyword. The keyword that contributed the most to revenue, at 17.4%, was ‘learn data analytics’, amounting to $643,359. Meanwhile, the keyword that contributed the least was ‘data analytics online’, accounting for 15.7% or the equivalent of $579,245. The difference between the two was only $64,114. |

### Recommendation

Here are some recommendations for conducting an in-depth analysis to gain a deeper understanding of current trends.
1. To investigate whether there is a correlation between the increase in advertising costs and the amount of revenue generated.
2. Analysing device and keyword performance based on marketing funnel aspects to identify which areas are underperforming, so that these can be optimised to maximise revenue.
