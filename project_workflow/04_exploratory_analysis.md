# Exploratory Analysis

Exploratory analysis is the stage for getting to know the data, identifying initial patterns, detecting anomalies or outliers, and formulating hypotheses.

### Requirements Gathering

Collecting requirements at this stage is done to make the analysis more focused. Exploratory
analysis is an initial effort to understand how ad performance has fared over the course of  year. The goal is to identify specific patterns in the data and gain insight into past trends. 

#### Purpose of the Analysis

To examine marketing performance through Google Ads based on revenue generated over time
to determine whether there was an increase or decrease in revenue during that year, as well as which dimensions had the greatest impact on that outcome.

#### Metrics and Dimensions

This exploratory analysis focuses on cost performance, which includes expenses and revenue
from marketing efforts through the Google Ads channel. Therefore, the metrics used in this
analysis are Cost and Sale_Amount. The dimensions used will be Ad_Date, Device, and Keyword.

#### Scope of the Analysis

The desired outcome of this exploratory phase is to identify revenue trends generated during ad campaigns.

- Performance patterns or trends in ad impressions, so we can determine when the company
experiences its highest and lowest revenue and how this compares to its costs. The metrics
and dimensions used are Cost and Sale_Amount, while the dimension used is Ad_Date. 
- The device types with the best and worst performance based on the Sale_Amount metric. 
- The best- and worst-performing keywords based on the Sale_Amount metric.

### Analysis Result

#### A. Ad Spend Performance (Cost and Revenue)

![cost_revenue_by_month]()

- Advertising costs in November were significantly higher than in previous months.
Typically, they range from $4,000 to $6,000, but this month they surged 77-fold to
$478,448. However, this increase in spending also boosted revenue by 78-fold. Average
revenue in other months was only $37,283, while in November it surged to $3,278,058.
- After seeing an increase in both expenses and revenue in November, both expenses and
revenue dropped by 99% in December. Advertising costs for the month fell sharply, with
a difference of $473,068 at . As a result, revenue totaled only $38,295—a decrease of at
least $3.2 million from the previous month. 
- The lowest revenue was recorded in July, at just $24,141. Advertising expenses for that
month were also among the lowest, at $4,145.

#### B. Device and Keyword Performance

![desktop_performance]()

In general, there is no significant difference in the revenue generated by customers who use
different devices to view ads.

People who view ads on desktop devices contribute the most revenue, at 34.3% or $1,264,233.
Meanwhile, those who access ads via tablets contribute the least, at about 32.2% or $1,188,131.

The difference between the two is only 6.4%, which is not significant. 

![keyword_performnace]()

Just as with the revenue for each keyword, there isn’t a significant difference.  

The keyword that contributed the most to revenue—17.4%—was “learn data analytics,” at
$643,359. Meanwhile, the keyword that contributed the least was “data analytics online,” which accounted for 15.7% or $579,245.

The difference between the two was only 11.1%.

#### C. Devices and Keywords That Most Influenced the Revenue Surge in November

![device_by_month]()

Just as the device performance rankings that contributed the most to overall revenue, the
device performance driving the revenue surge in November was also dominated by desktop
users, who accounted for 34.4% of that month’s revenue.

![keyword_by_month]()

Keyword performance in November was also roughly the same as the rankings of the
keywords that had the greatest impact on total revenue, with the keyword “learn data
analytics” contributing the most to revenue that month, at 17.4%.

The only difference lies in the second and third positions among the largest contributors
overall and specifically in November.

In November, the keyword “online data analytics” (third-ranked contributor to total
revenue) ranked second with a 17.1% share, while the keyword “data analytics course”
(second-ranked contributor to total revenue) ranked third   with a 16.8% share of that
month’s revenue.

### Recommendation

After conducting an exploratory analysis to identify revenue trends and the factors influencing them, here are the recommended analyses that need to be performed to explain the causes of some of the previous findings.

1. To determine the cause of the spike in revenue in November and the subsequent sharp 
decline in December. 
2. Determining whether there is a correlation between the increase in advertising expenses 
and the amount of revenue generated. 
3. Determining the reasons for the high revenue generated from desktop devices and the use 
of the keyword “learn data analytics.”
