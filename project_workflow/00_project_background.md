# Project Background

This project is a piece of work I created based on the results of a structured analysis using data representative of real-world conditions. This project demonstrates my ability to understand, prepare, analyze, and transform data into insights that result in actionable business plans that can be immediately implemented. 

This project will examine the performance of previous digital marketing efforts and their impact on the company’s revenue. By using freely accessible datasets combined with an understanding of the business and industry, the analysis will yield findings regarding advertising performance and the revenue generated from these efforts.

This project will be divided into several phases before generating business recommendations.

1. Understanding the data and business context

This stage is the most important one. The data will be defined based on what actually occurs in the field, and from there, it will become clear what information can be derived from that data. Additionally, the data will be tailored to meet business needs. The data will be evaluated to determine whether it aligns with the business context and whether it can yield relevant business insights.

Since this is an independent project, establishing the business context is done independently while still referencing real-world business conditions.

2. Preparing the data

This stage will further demonstrate technical proficiency in data processing. Data obtained from any source must be checked for quality. Data quality will significantly influence the results of the analysis. Poor-quality data will yield inaccurate findings. Therefore, it is important to check data quality and clean up problematic data.

3. Data analysis

Once the data has been cleaned, the analysis can begin. At this stage, the data is processed based on the previously defined objectives of the analysis. This enables the generation of findings that can describe customer behavior or past phenomena that have impacted the business.

4. Formulating business recommendations

After understanding the business situation as it stood previously, the next step is to formulate recommendations. Based on the understanding of the business context established earlier and the findings obtained from data analysis, proposals regarding what can be done moving forward can be presented. These recommendations depend heavily on the audience for whom they are intended.

---

Each stage will be organized into separate files and placed in the same folder.

The final deliverables of this project consist of business findings and recommendations, an interactive dashboard summarizing the findings, and a project reflection containing key notes from throughout the project.
