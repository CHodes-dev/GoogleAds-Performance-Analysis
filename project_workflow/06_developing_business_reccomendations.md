### B. Recommendation

Creating ads on the Google Ads platform is part of the TOFU (Top of the Funnel) strategy and
falls under the category of reach ads, whose purpose is to capture existing demand. Thus, when people enter the trigger keyword, the ad will be displayed.

The chances of an ad being displayed on Google Ads depend on two factors: Ad Rank, which determines whether an ad is eligible to win the auction and where it appears in search results, and the ad budget (bid), which determines how often the ad is allowed to participate in the auction throughout the day. 

To maximize ad performance on Google Ads, marketing analysts need to report on the
performance of previously run ads to identify which areas are performing well and which
need improvement, as well as recommend steps that can be taken to improve performance.

Afterward, the Media Planner and Content Strategist develop a strategic plan based on the
recommendations provided for future ad campaigns.

1. Set a daily budget for ad placements 

Ads that appear every day on the search results pages of your target audience are more likely to be clicked and generate conversions than ads that appear only one day a month. This is important to do to increase revenue from conversions made by the audience.

2. Increase the budget for ad placements

Increasing your budget, particularly by paying a higher cost per click (CPC) under certain
conditions, can ensure your ads appear at the top of your audience’s search results pages. Here are some keywords for which you might want to increase your budget.

- “data analytics training” (Top 5 keyword) <br>
This keyword is the most efficient, so increasing the ad budget for it can boost the number of impressions, thereby significantly increasing the likelihood of clicks and conversions. 
- 'learn data analytics' (Top 1 keyword) <br>
As the keyword that generates the highest revenue, increasing the budget for this keyword is not a top priority. Although it is not one of the most efficient keywords for generating conversions, increasing its budget could boost revenue.

3. Improve ad and landing page quality

To improve Ad Rank so that ads appear at the top of the rankings without having to raise the cost-per-click (CPC) bid, you can also optimize the Quality Score, which is assessed based on Ad Relevance, how relevant the ad is to the user and Landing Page Experience, the user’s experience while on the landing page.

Here are some steps you can take to improve the quality of your ads and landing pages based on the devices your audience uses to access them.

- Mobile (Top 2 Devices) <br>
As a device with high potential for increasing revenue, improving the quality of the landing pages displayed on these devices can help achieve that goal. <br>
Using short paragraphs and improving load speed are effective strategies to adapt to the user experience on these devices, where users expect instant access to information. 
- Desktop (Top 1 device) <br>
Before improving the quality of the landing page for this device, the main focus is on making ad impressions more targeted. The high number of impressions for this type of ad does not translate to a corresponding number of people who actually click on the ad. 
- Tablets (last-rank device) <br>
Ads of this type need improvement in both ad display and landing page quality. Ad quality can be improved by optimizing the text specifically for tablet screen ratios, while the quality of landing pages displayed on these medium-sized screens can be improved by optimizing content layout and user navigation.

Regarding keyword usage, here are the steps we can take.

- “data analytics course” (Top 2 keyword) <br>
This keyword has high potential and should be prioritized for use in ad copy and landing page text. Since it demonstrates high efficiency in driving conversions, an increase in the number of conversions will quickly boost revenue from these ads.
- 'learn data analytics' (Top 1 keyword) <br>
Actually, improving rankings for these keywords isn’t the main priority, but to maximize revenue from their use, it’s necessary to align the text on the landing page with the needs of users who search for these keywords.
- “online data analytics” (Top 3 keyword) and “data analytics online” (lowest-ranking 
keyword) <br>
The relevance of the ads to users arriving via these keywords is already quite good; to increase the number of conversions, it’s important to ensure the landing page contains messaging that aligns with what these two keywords indicate.
- 'analytics for data' (Top 4 keyword) <br>
The message conveyed in the ads that appear when users enter this keyword needs to be reviewed and tailored to users’ needs. Additionally, the message on the landing page must also be tailored to this keyword so that users perceive the services offered as relevant to their needs.
